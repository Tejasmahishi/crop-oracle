How to take a good photo
  • ☀️Use daylight. Do not use the flash.
  • 🍃Fill the frame with one leaf that shows the spots.
  • 🎯Hold the phone steady and tap the leaf so it is in focus.
  • 🖐️Keep your shadow and your fingers off the leaf.
  • 🔄If you can, take a second photo of the underside of the leaf.

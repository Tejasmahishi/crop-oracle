In short: We do not keep your photo unless you tick the box that allows it. If you do, we keep the photo and a rough location (accurate to about 11 km). We never ask for your name or phone number. You can delete it at any time.

What happens when you analyse a photo

  • Your photo is sent to our server, measured, and then thrown away. It is not saved unless you allow it (see below).
  • The place you choose (or your phone's location, if you allow it) is used to look up the weather. The page sends it to Open-Meteo and BigDataCloud, and our server sends the same coordinates to them as well.
    • {{KINDWISE_ITEM}}
  • What you type in the symptoms box is used only for that analysis and is not stored.
  • The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees that your device opened the page.
  • Reading the advice aloud uses the voices installed on your own phone; nothing is sent for that.

"Was this right?" answers

After a result you can tell us whether it was right. This always stores: your answer, what the app said, the crop, the language, the app version, the photo-quality warnings and the date.

Only if you tick "You may keep my photo and rough location to improve this app" we also store the photo (shrunk, and with the camera's hidden data such as GPS removed) and your location rounded to about 11 km.

We do not store your name, phone number or IP address with the answer.

The stored photos are used only to check and improve disease recognition for crops in Karnataka, for example by agricultural experts labelling them. They are not sold or published.

How long we keep it

Answers and photos are deleted automatically after {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days (0 means they are kept until you or we delete them).

Delete your answer and photo

After you send an answer, the app shows you a record number and a delete code. Enter both here to delete that answer and its photo.

If you lost the code, write to us (below) and describe roughly when you sent the answer. Because we do not keep your name or phone number, we may not be able to find it.
